7 Cs of Communication

1. Clear

Use simple and understandable language.

2. Concise

Keep your message brief and meaningful.

3. Concrete

Use specific facts and examples.

4. Correct

Use accurate grammar and information.

5. Coherent

Arrange ideas in a logical order.

6. Complete

Include all necessary information.

7. Courteous

Be polite and respectful.

Grammar Basics

Parts of Speech

Noun: Name of a person, place or thing.

Pronoun: Used instead of a noun.

Verb: Shows an action or state.

Adjective: Describes a noun.

Adverb: Describes a verb, adjective or another adverb.

Preposition: Shows a relationship between words.

Conjunction: Joins words or sentences.

Interjection: Expresses a sudden feeling.
